About

mementomorikc is a photographer and visual artist working at the intersection of portraiture, fashion and conceptual storytelling. The work centres on concealed faces, heritage textiles and staged surreal moments — images that ask what remains of a person when the face is taken away.

Available for editorial, commercial and personal commissions.
